4审批决策状态机

approvalQueue: ApprovalRequest[]
       │
       ▼ approvalQueue[0] (FIFO)
       │
       ├── dangerous_tool
       │     ├── 拒绝 (deny)
       │     ├── 批准 (approve)
       │     └── autoApproveAfterSeconds > 0 → 倒计时归零自动 approve
       │
       └── directory_extension
             ├── 拒绝 (deny)
             ├── 本次允许 (once) - 调用结束后失效
             └── 会话内允许 (session) - 整个会话允许访问该路径

       ▼
approval.submit(threadId, approved, decision, path?, writable?)
       │
       ▼
dequeueApprovalRequest() → 渲染下一个
